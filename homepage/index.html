Betting Feature Store

Apache Flink + Apache Fluss · a real-time feature store

A sports-betting platform that is already trading: bets, settlements, logins and deposits stream into Kafka, players and wallets change in PostgreSQL. The events land in Fluss log tables, tiered to an Iceberg lake for point-in-time history. Seven Flink SQL jobs turn them into one feature row per player in a Fluss primary-key table, and every new bet looks that row up the moment it arrives, is scored by risk rules, and alerts land in PostgreSQL and Grafana. Adding a feature later bootstraps it from the lake.
Two modes: ./start.sh runs all of it (complete); in ./start.sh --simple the feature jobs read the Kafka topics directly — no ingest, no Fluss log tables, no lake, no added feature.

The steps

  1. Declare the sources The fluss catalog, two postgres-cdc tables and the Kafka topics. flink_sql/01_sources_and_catalogs.sql
  2. Create the Fluss tables The online feature store, a Fluss primary-key table. flink_sql/02_fluss_feature_store.sql
  3. Ingest and tier Complete mode: four tiered Fluss log tables, one job copying the topics into them, and the tiering service copying Fluss to Iceberg. Simple mode skips this step. flink_sql/03_ingest_to_fluss.sql, scripts/tiering.sh
  4. Fill the feature store Job A streams the dimensions from PostgreSQL; B1–D compute per-event and pattern features from the Fluss logs, each into its own columns. flink_sql/04_cdc_dimensions.sql, flink_sql/05_feature_calculations.sql
  5. Score every bet A lookup join FOR SYSTEM_TIME AS OF proc_time into Fluss, rules, the risk_scores topic, then the alerts to PostgreSQL. flink_sql/06_risk_scoring_inference.sql
  6. Fire an outlier and look The outlier generator, the inference API, Grafana, and the ad hoc and point-in-time queries. flink_sql/07_analytics_and_monitoring.sql
  7. Add a feature (complete mode) ALTER TABLE adds avg_stake_7d to the live store; one job bootstraps it from the Iceberg history and carries on with live bets. flink_sql/08_add_feature_avg_stake.sql
